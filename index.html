What the link opens

The link carries this piece's parameters, colors, font, speed and the current moment t. To send several moments with a sentence each, of this piece or of several, add each to a list with Add this moment (also under Save and Customize): one link plays the list.

Click, Enter
On a piece marked “Click to interact”: hand the mouse and keys to the piece (tap it on a phone). It shows what to do, such as dragging to orbit and scrolling to zoom

          
Esc, click outside
Give them back to the page: scrolling, time scrubbing, long-press and these shortcuts return. Moving to another slide or opening a panel does the same

          
While interacting
Drag, right-drag, the scroll wheel and keys other than Esc, Tab and Ctrl/⌘ shortcuts go to the piece; on a phone one finger drags, two fingers pinch to zoom, a double tap is a double-click
